img1-Cervical discharge in gonorrhea (speculum view)
The surface of the cervix is covered with yellowish-white, purulent discharge. The external os of the uterus is inflamed and red.
These findings are consistent with gonorrhea.

img2-First trimester ultrasound, double sac sign
Ultrasound pelvis (gestational sac)
The embryo (green overlay), or fetal pole, measures 3 mm, which corresponds to an estimated gestational age of approximately 5 weeks. The yolk sac (red overlay) is visible adjacent to the embryo.
Blue overlay: double sac sign; E: endometrium

img3-Tubal ring sign (blob sign) of ectopic pregnancy
Ultrasound pelvis (transvaginal; oblique plane)
In the right adnexa, an ectopic pregnancy is visible as a rounded mass adjacent to the right ovary. Echoes are seen within fluid in the peritoneal space, indicating hemoperitoneum with coagulated blood. A small hyperechoic linear structure located centrally within the endometrial cavity represents the visible portion of an IUD.

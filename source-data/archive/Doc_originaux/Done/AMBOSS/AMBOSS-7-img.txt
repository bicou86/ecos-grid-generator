img2-Sunken eyes in dehydration
10-year old boy
The acute presentation of sunken eyes, especially in association with altered mental status or unconsciousness, should raise suspicion for potentially life-threatening dehydration.
The cause for dehydration in this child was diarrhea due to typhoid fever.

img3-Severe dehydration in an infant
Abdomen of an infant: After pinching, the abdominal skin recoils slowly, showing significant skin tenting. This may be a sign of severe dehydration, e.g., in the course of a gastrointestinal infection.
